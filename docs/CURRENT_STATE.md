# Current state

Chapters I–III of «Сушь» are playable: the Rusty Well, the world map of Низовье, Три столба, Колючка, the barge «Стрежень», and the city of Запруда with five areas. Towns are made of several areas, linked by ways out and entered from the world map through a town plan. Online co-op and the arena work (with a game server). 416 unit and 22 end-to-end tests pass. The single-player game is published at https://sweetiki56-prog.github.io/sush/.

`PROJECT_STATE.md` is the live record: what is done, where to continue, and the handoff notes. See `STORY.md` for the story and locations, `DOMAIN.md` for the rules, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` for rooms and the server.
