# Current state

Chapters I and II of «Сушь» are playable. The Rusty Well, Три столба and Колючка are towns of several areas, and the barge «Стрежень» has three areas of its own. They are linked by ways out and entered from the world map through a town plan, with the relic quests of stage R and the area quests of stage L. Online co-op and the arena work. 356 unit and 20 end-to-end tests pass.

`PROJECT_STATE.md` is the live record: what is done, where to continue, and the handoff notes. See `STORY.md` for the story and locations, `DOMAIN.md` for the rules, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` for rooms and the server.
