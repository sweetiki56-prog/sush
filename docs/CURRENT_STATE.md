# Current state

Chapters I–VI of «Сушь» are playable, and companions walk with the hero: the Rusty Well, the world map of Низовье and the Солончаки, Три столба, Колючка, the barge «Стрежень», the city of Запруда with five areas, the Guild city of Соль with four and the Кладбище судов with the snake's lair, Кристалл with four, the Скит of the Орден Росы with four and the station «Роса-2» with three. Towns are made of several areas, linked by ways out and entered from the world map through a town plan. Online co-op and the arena work (with a game server). 586 unit and 25 end-to-end tests pass. The game is playable on phones by touch (landscape). The single-player game is published at https://sweetiki56-prog.github.io/sush/.

`PROJECT_STATE.md` is the live record: what is done, where to continue, and the handoff notes. See `STORY.md` for the story and locations, `DOMAIN.md` for the rules, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` for rooms and the server.
