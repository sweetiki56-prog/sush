# Current state

The whole main story of «Сушь» — Chapters I–IX — is playable to its ending slides, and companions walk with the hero: the Rusty Well, the world map of Низовье, the Солончаки and the Верховья, Три столба, Колючка, the barge «Стрежень», the city of Запруда with five areas, the Guild city of Соль with four and the Кладбище судов with the snake's lair, Кристалл with four, the Скит of the Орден Росы with four, the station «Роса-2» with three, the Депо «Узловое» with four, the relay «Шептун» with three, the pass «Ворота», the hidden Орлиное гнездо, the Dead fields, the shelter of Тишина, the Elevator and the Ark of Низовье, the Костяной круг of the Сухари with three areas, the ruins of Светлоречье with its hidden Chamber, the Watcher's bunker, the treasury train «Литерный», the last courier's station, and the dam «Заслон». S15a adds the twelfth seal, four new trial pieces and «Сухие руки»; S15b remains. Towns are made of several areas, linked by ways out and entered from the world map through a town plan. Online co-op and the arena work (with a game server). 784 unit and 30 end-to-end tests pass. The game is playable on phones by touch (landscape). The single-player game is published at https://sweetiki56-prog.github.io/sush/.

`PROJECT_STATE.md` is the live record: what is done, where to continue, and the handoff notes. See `STORY.md` for the story and locations, `DOMAIN.md` for the rules, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` for rooms and the server.
